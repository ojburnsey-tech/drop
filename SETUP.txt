Drop: updating your iPhone app

1. Open your "drop" repository on github.com.
2. Click Add file, then Upload files.
3. Drag in all six files: index.html, app.css, app.js, scanner.js, bg.jpg, apple-touch-icon.png
   (replace the old ones when asked), then click Commit changes.
4. Wait about a minute for GitHub Pages to update.

Getting the full-screen app (no Safari address bar)

5. On your Chromebook, open a new tab, click the phone button, choose Drop, then Pairing code.
6. Scan the code with your iPhone camera and open the link in Safari.
7. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Make sure Open as Web App is switched on, then tap Add.
8. Delete any older Drop icon from your Home Screen, and always open the new one.

If the app ever asks you to connect: in Safari, open Drop's Settings, tap Copy next to Pairing link,
then paste it into the box on the Connect screen of the Home Screen app.

Share sheet shortcuts

In Drop, tap the gear, then "How to build the shortcuts". The two addresses you need are on that
screen with Copy buttons.
