Drop: your iPhone send page

Put this page on GitHub Pages (free, about 5 minutes, one time only)

1. Go to github.com and sign in.
2. Click the + at the top right, then New repository.
3. Name it: drop
   Leave it Public (GitHub Pages is only free for public repositories; the page contains nothing private).
   Click Create repository.
4. On the next screen, click "uploading an existing file".
5. Drag in these five files: index.html, app.css, app.js, bg.jpg, apple-touch-icon.png
   Click Commit changes.
6. Open the repository's Settings tab, then Pages in the left sidebar.
   Under "Build and deployment", set Source to "Deploy from a branch",
   Branch to "main" and folder to "/ (root)", then click Save.
7. Wait about a minute and refresh. GitHub shows your address at the top of the Pages screen:
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/drop/

Connect it to your Chromebook

8. Open a new tab, click the phone button in the bottom-left, choose From iPhone,
   paste your address and click Save.
9. Point your iPhone camera at the QR code that appears and tap the link.
10. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from your Home Screen from now on.

Privacy

Your private channel name travels in the part of the QR link after the # sign,
which browsers never send to GitHub. If you ever want to cut off old pairings,
use Reset pairing in the new tab settings and scan the new code.
